# News coverage

When news sentiment is enabled, ADAM searches Bing News and attempts to retrieve recent articles from CoinCentral and Blockonomi. It does not follow article redirects or fetch arbitrary hosts. It checks each publisher's `robots.txt` and skips articles if the policy cannot be retrieved or uses unsupported wildcard rules; a missing policy (HTTP 404) permits retrieval. Matching disallow rules are applied conservatively, even when they target another user agent. Articles must mention the ticker, but a mention does not prove relevance. At most five readable articles are scored and duplicate text is excluded. ADAM averages FinBERT scores over 800-character chunks, capped at 20,000 characters per article, then weights each article equally. FinBERT may still truncate individual chunks. Short extracts, heuristic financial highlights, source links, chunk counts, and a text-truncation flag are kept in the analysis history. Highlights are copied sentences selected by keywords and numbers; they are not verified facts.

If no usable article is found or article discovery is unavailable, ADAM searches Google News and scores recent headlines instead. The result identifies whether it used articles or headlines. Coverage is narrow, search results can be unrelated, and publisher availability can change; missing news causes that agent to abstain. Neither sentiment nor a summary establishes a news event's investment impact.
